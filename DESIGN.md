# Locked PDP design context

- Direction: Nordic Ember
- Personality: warm, calm, trustworthy, evening ritual
- Palette: Night pine `#17251F`, Lantern cream `#F5F0E6`, Ember gold `#C9852B`, Warm white `#FFF9EA`, Moss grey `#68736B`
- Typography: Georgia for display headings; Arial for body and controls
- Components: subtle borders, 14px radii, soft warm elevation, high-contrast radio offer cards
- Hero: product-led gallery left and purchase decision right on desktop; gallery-first single column on mobile
- Media: preserve approved generated assets, lantern geometry, scale and readable Danish on-image arguments
- Offer: recommended gift bundle must remain visually dominant without changing price, savings or quantities
- Motion: one subtle arrival moment plus restrained interaction feedback; respect reduced-motion preference
- Section order: promotion, brand header, hero, how it works, benefits, product details, comparison, FAQ, final offer, footer
